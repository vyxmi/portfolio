# Portfolio design constitution

Established 2026-09-05 from Vyomi's brief and subsequent decisions. The user brief remains authoritative; this document is its operational summary.

## Purpose

A professional portfolio and a personal place on the internet. Intrigue → credibility → personality → delight, without compromising finding work, reading, location or contact. Identity: problem solver, product designer, systems thinker, maker, creative person, engineer, startup operator. Demonstrate judgment through truthful evidence, not self-labels.

## Preserve / challenge

Preserve strong content, truthful evidence, Vyomi's voice, useful interaction ideas, and components that actually work. Current presentation is raw material, not specification. Typography, hierarchy, composition, images, motion and component styling are open to challenge. Existing fonts/palette are a working baseline, not sacred. Major font/color changes still require a concrete recommendation before adoption.

Establish a rigorous shared system, then pursue a few ambitious art-direction swings within it. Coherence is not normalization. Selection, scale, rhythm, relationships and withholding should create surprise; do not add unrelated effects or template polish.

## Fixed utility

Home contains all four selected projects, direct case-study entry, outcome-led previews, a small Brain preview after work, and contact. Persistent desktop contextual navigation; mobile retains navigation, context, state and contact. Email is one action away. Articles offer 15-second, 2-minute and full reading depths without flattening the prose. Brain remains abundant but intentionally published.

## Expression and motion

One or two major motifs. Approved direction: contextual navigable outline plus contained object behavior. State changes, spatial continuity and meaningful relationships take precedence over decorative movement. Reading and targets remain stable; reduced motion is honored. No new WebGL or physics system for V1.

## Evidence and voice

Never manufacture metrics, quotes, research, product screens or outcomes. The guessed $5,000 prevented-loss claim is withdrawn. No replacement support-ticket percentage without data. Deposit modal rejection came from teammates, not a 98% user experiment. Preserve actual measurement assumptions and uncertainty. Substantive narrative rewrites/cuts require exact proposals; factual corrections expressly supplied by Vyomi are authorized.

## Content system

Separate domains, relationship, type, tags, publication lifecycle, featured selection, related objects and rendering metadata. Public visibility is not database existence. Private data and media stay out of public responses/assets. Prefer structured local content and a lightweight editor; retain rich article content files. Preserve original source records.

## Scope and convergence

V1 includes stronger thumbnails, work cards, visuals and selective effects/GIFs/video. These must make real work/evidence easier to understand and evaluate. No obligation to make a video for each project. When recordings are unavailable, use truthful diagrams, static comparisons and available artifacts; never disguise simulated UI as a product recording.

New evidence or real problems can reopen decisions. New solutions, preferences and possibilities normally enter quarantine. High-impact, low/medium-tuning improvements first. No total rewrite by default, no sunk-cost preservation.

## Quality

Responsive design is intrinsic, with layout based on available content width. Semantic headings, keyboard/focus behavior, useful alt text, stable media, reduced motion and efficient loading are required. Test each batch and record outstanding limitations. Never treat a successful build as visual QA.
